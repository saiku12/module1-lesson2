Biography of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Introduction

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a great Indian scientist and the 11th President of India.

Early Life

He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Education

He studied physics and aerospace engineering.

Achievements

He played an important role in India's space and missile programmes.

Legacy

He is remembered as the Missile Man of India.
